For every future imported file: source URL, creator, asset name/version, retrieval date, archive hash, exact licence text/evidence, attribution obligations, modifications, original and normalized dimensions, source frame, and generated GLB hashes are mandatory. A free price or download button is insufficient. Reject unclear/redistribution-prohibited licences; attribution-required assets need shipped credits and licence text. Do not assume one site's licence covers a linked third-party upload. Avoid NC/ND assets for this catalogue.

The current original-asset licence label records that external distribution terms remain the owner's choice; it is not an assertion that an unknown external rights holder must approve these locally authored meshes.
